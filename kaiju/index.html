HOW TO PLAY

You are a kaiju. Come ashore, complete the objective, and earn biomass to grow stronger or awaken new titans.

WASD / left stickMove (camera relative). Hold Shift / LB to run.
Mouse / right stickLook. Scroll to zoom.
Left mouse / XAttack. Press again to chain a combo.
Right mouse / YHeavy attack.
Q, F, middle mouse / LTSignature power. Hold to channel; it aims where you look.
Space / AStomp or leap.
E / BRoar: panics the city and rattles the military.
T / right stick clickMutation Surge, once the mutagen meter is full.
Esc / StartPause, settings, retreat.

Buildings are real structures: knock out the lower floors and the floors above will fall. Tall towers topple into their neighbours. Fires spread and weaken what they burn.

Mutation. In the lab, the Mutation tab rewrites a kaiju into a new strain (Infernal, Glacial, Venom, Titan or Void), with its own look, stats and an element in every blow. Genes are traits you slot into any kaiju, three at a time. In a rampage, destruction fills the mutagen meter; when it is full, press T to surge into a bigger, faster, tougher state for a while.

The military escalates with the damage you cause. Tanks hold the avenues, helicopters circle, and jets make strafing runs. Swat them or burn them out of the sky.
